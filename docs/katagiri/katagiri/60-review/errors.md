# Error museum

Every mistake you make, kept on purpose. After two months this is a syllabus written specifically for you — one no course could produce, because no course knows what *you* get wrong.

Log format: what you said → what was right → the pattern → status.

| Date | I said | Correct | Pattern | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-08-18 | "de-su" | "des" | devoiced final う | active |
| 2026-08-18 | read は as "ha" | わ as topic marker | は reading | active |

## Patterns to watch

Once a pattern appears three times, it graduates to a **targeted drill** — and the system generates sentences designed to re-trap it, so you meet the trap deliberately instead of by accident. Adversarial practice beats hopeful practice.

- `devoiced-vowels` — 0 occurrences → drill at 3
- `wa-topic-reading` — 1
- `mora-length` — 0

## Fossilization watch

Errors still `active` after 30 days get flagged here. Fossilized errors are the reason people plateau at "understandable but foreign" for a decade. Catching one at week 3 costs minutes; at year 3 it costs months.
