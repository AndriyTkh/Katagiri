# Fruit & Vegetables

Fruit is counted with 〜つ (ひとつ、ふたつ…) unless it's small and round, in which case 〜個（こ）is common too. Both are understood.

## Sentences

- これは 林檎です。 — This is an apple. `polite` →[[g-desu-copula]] →[[g-wa-topic]]
- 林檎を 食べます。 — I eat an apple. `polite`
- 水を のみます。 — I drink water. `polite`

## Notes

### 林檎（りんご）
Almost always written in kana in real life; 林檎 is decorative. Heiban, so り↗んご stays level and a following particle stays high too.

Not アップル — that's the company or a brand name, never the fruit.

### 水（みず）
Means **cold** water specifically. Hot water is a completely different word: お湯（おゆ）. Asking for 水 in a café when you wanted tea-temperature water gets you ice water, correctly.

In a restaurant: お水（おみず）をください。 The お is politeness, not part of the word.
