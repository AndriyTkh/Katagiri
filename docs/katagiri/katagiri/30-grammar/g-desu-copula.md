# です — "is / am / are" (polite)

## Shape

`[noun] です。`

- これは りんご**です**。 — This is an apple.
- わたしは がくせい**です**。 — I am a student.

## The three things that actually matter

**1. It is pronounced "des", not "de-su".**
The final う is devoiced. This is the very first thing that marks a beginner's accent. Same with ます → "mas".

**2. It is politeness, not meaning.**
Casual Japanese drops it: これは りんご。 Same meaning, different social register. It is not "wrong" — it's what you'll hear constantly in anime, and what you should *not* use with a shopkeeper.

**3. It does not conjugate like a verb.**

| | polite | casual |
|---|---|---|
| is | です | だ |
| was | でした | だった |
| is not | じゃありません / ではありません | じゃない |
| was not | じゃありませんでした | じゃなかった |

## Register note

だ is blunt and sounds masculine/assertive in speech; women's casual speech often drops the copula entirely rather than using だ. Anime will give you だぜ and だろ — flag those as `anime-only`.
