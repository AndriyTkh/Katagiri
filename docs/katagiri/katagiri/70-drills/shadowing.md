# Shadowing

The highest-return ten minutes in your day, and it requires no comprehension at all — which is why it works from week one.

## Protocol

1. Pick 15–30 seconds of native audio with a transcript.
2. **Listen only.** Three times. Don't repeat yet.
3. **Mumble along** while listening — same rhythm, no clear words. This trains timing before articulation.
4. **Full shadow** with the text visible: speak *simultaneously*, not after.
5. **Shadow without text.**
6. Record yourself. Listen back. Note one specific thing to fix (not five).

## What to attend to, in order

1. **Rhythm** — one beat per mora ([[35-phonology/mora-timing]])
2. **Pitch contour** — where the voice drops ([[35-phonology/pitch-accent]])
3. **Devoicing** — des, mas, ski
4. Only then individual consonants

Most people do 4 first and never fix 1 or 2. Rhythm and pitch carry more intelligibility than any single consonant.

## Variants

- **Shadow-dub** — mute the original line, dub it, compare pitch curves overlaid. §3.5 in [[ARCHITECTURE]].
- **Karaoke** — Japanese songs enforce mora timing by construction (one mora per note) and give you forty willing repetitions. Underrated as serious prosody training.
- **Speed ladder** — 0.75× → 1.0× → 1.25×. The fast pass makes normal speed feel slow.
