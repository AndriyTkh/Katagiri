# Media

One note per video / episode / chapter / song. This is where immersion becomes data.

## Before you watch anything

```bash
katagiri coverage --url <link>
```

Returns: % of tokens you know, the unknown words ranked by frequency *within this work*, and the short list of words that would take you from (say) 74% → 91%. Pre-learn those, then watch. That gap between "incomprehensible" and "comprehensible" is usually 10–20 words wide, and finding them is the whole trick.

## Rules

- Subtitle files, audio, and video go in `local/` (gitignored). Never in the repo.
- Media notes hold: reference, your timestamped notes, mined items, and short quoted lines only.
- Register-tag everything you mine from fiction. Anime dialogue is a goldmine and a social minefield.
