# The sounds that will betray you

Targeted problems, not general practice.

> **Read [[l1-profile]] first.** This page is the generic English-speaker list. Three items on it are *free* for you because of Ukrainian, and it omits your two biggest actual risks (rounded う, and dynamic stress corrupting pitch accent). The profile has your real priority order.

## ら行 — not L, not R  ✅ free for you (Ukrainian р)

ら り る れ ろ are a single **alveolar flap**: tongue tip taps the ridge behind the teeth once, lightly. Closest English reference is the tt in American "butter." Substituting English R is the most recognisable foreign accent in Japanese.

## ふ — no teeth  ⚠️ target — you have ф and х, neither is [ɸ]

ふ is **bilabial** — air between the lips, like blowing out a candle. Not the English F (lip against teeth).

## つ — one sound  ✅ free for you (Ukrainian ц)

ts as a single onset. English only has it word-finally ("cats"), so "tsu" wants to become "tu" or "su". Practise つ、つき、つくえ.

## Devoiced vowels  ⚠️ target — no L1 support

- です → "des"
- ます → "mas"
- 好き（すき）→ "ski"
- ~しました → "shimashta"

Fully pronouncing these u/i vowels is the textbook-beginner giveaway.

## が mid-word

Often nasalised in Tokyo speech (closer to "nga"). Optional, but hearing it helps you parse.

## ん assimilates

ん takes the shape of what follows: こんばんは (m before b), こんにちは (n), ほんかん (ng before k). Don't force one sound.
