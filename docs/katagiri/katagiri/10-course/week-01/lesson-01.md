# Lesson 01 — Your first four words and two rules

*(Rōmaji is included in weeks 1–2 only, then it disappears. See [[CONVENTIONS]].)*

## 1. The two pronunciation rules that matter more than any word

**Every mora gets equal time.** おかあさん is five beats, not four. Tap them out. ([[35-phonology/mora-timing]])

**Pitch drops, stress doesn't.** はし with a drop after mora 1 is chopsticks; after mora 2 it's a bridge. ([[35-phonology/pitch-accent]])

You will not master these today. You need to know they exist before you learn 500 words the wrong way.

## 3. Grammar

**[[g-desu-copula]]** — です = polite "is". Say it **"des."** Not "de-su."

**[[g-wa-topic]]** — は marks the topic. Written は, **pronounced わ**. Always.

## 4. Say these out loud, five times each

1. これは りんごです。 — *Kore wa ringo desu.* — This is an apple.
2. これは 水です。 — *Kore wa mizu desu.* — This is water.
3. りんごを 食べます。 — *Ringo o tabemasu.* — I eat an apple.

Out loud. Not in your head. The output clock only counts what leaves your mouth.
