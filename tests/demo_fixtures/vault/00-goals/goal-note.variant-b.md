# Current goal

I want to be able to get from a train station to somewhere I've never
been without opening a map app: ask someone which platform or exit to
use, understand a short spoken answer, and ask them to repeat or slow
down if I miss it.

## Why this, right now

Transport vocabulary and direction-giving grammar (*e*-direction, *made*-
"as far as", polite requests to repeat) come up constantly while
travelling and are almost never drilled together. This theme lets the
same session cover "excuse me, where is ___" and "turn left at ___" as
one connected thread instead of two disconnected word lists.

## What "done" looks like

- Ask "where is the ___ exit / platform" (どこですか) and follow a short
  spoken answer.
- Ask someone to repeat or speak more slowly (もう一度 / ゆっくり) without
  switching to English.
- Understand basic transport signage vocabulary (platform, exit,
  transfer, ticket gate) on sight.
