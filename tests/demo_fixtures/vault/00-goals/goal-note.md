# Current goal

I want to be able to walk into a small restaurant and order confidently
without switching to English: read the menu, ask what something is, ask
for a recommendation, and say what I can't eat.

## Why this, right now

Food vocabulary comes up in almost every lesson already, and it is the
fastest way to get a real conversation with a stranger to go well. Grammar
points I already half-know (*wa*-topic, *o*-object, polite requests) all
show up naturally in "may I have the ___" and "what is in ___" — so this
theme should let me drill grammar and vocabulary at the same time instead
of as separate exercises.

## What "done" looks like

- Order a full meal (starter, main, drink) without hesitating on a word.
- Ask "what is this" (これは何ですか) about an unfamiliar menu item and
  understand the answer well enough to decide.
- Say a dietary restriction or preference without switching to English.
